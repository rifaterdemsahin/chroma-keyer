✅ This way vs DaVinci on an M1

🧠 Why External Pre-Processing?

This local “divide and conquer” workflow works better than keeping the key inside DaVinci Resolve — especially on an M1 Mac Mini. You still can finish in Resolve; you just do not make Resolve compute the key on every playhead move.

Use Resolve when you need its color page. Use this repo when you want a keyed, Canva-ready clip without melting the M1 on Magic Mask.

1. Eliminates Resolve Timeline GPU Throttling

When you run Resolve’s built-in 3D Keyer, Delta Keyer, or Magic Mask directly on the timeline, Resolve re-calculates or pulls that mask every single frame you scrub, edit, or play back.

  • The Resolve Bottleneck: On an M1 Mac Mini, running complex AI spatial/temporal neural networks while Resolve renders color nodes, audio playback, and timeline UI often forces the system down to 1–5 FPS.
  • The Offload Solution: By batch-processing the alpha mask externally to a static file (PNG/EXR sequence or ProRes alpha video), DaVinci Resolve treats the mask as simple video data. Playback instantly locks back to a smooth 24/30 FPS with near-zero GPU load.

2. Purpose-Built AI vs. Broad-Purpose Rotoscoping

DaVinci Resolve’s Magic Mask is an all-purpose object segmenter (designed to catch people, cars, objects, and foregrounds across complex scenes).

  • Specialized matting models like Robust Video Matting (RVM) are explicitly trained for human-over-background video matting.
  • RVM uses recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to remember temporal continuity across frames. This eliminates the flickering or edge "chatter" around fine hair strands, fingers, and motion-blurred edges that often plague Resolve keyers.

3. Solves the "Garbage Matte" Problem Programmatically

In DaVinci Resolve, removing room clutter (like your side curtains, artwork, or wall edges) requires manually drawing power windows, keyframing garbage mattes, or setting up complex Fusion nodes.

  • Doing this programmatic crop before AI inference crops away the noise first.
  • The AI engine only processes the clean green bounds, reducing frame resolution fed to the neural network. This slashes VRAM usage and speeds up processing time dramatically.

4. Apple Silicon MPS & Neural Engine Utilization

When running RVM via Python or WebGPU, execution targets the Metal Performance Shaders (MPS) framework directly on your M1 chip.

  • It bypasses the massive VRAM overhead of DaVinci Resolve’s full rendering engine.
  • Dedicated scripts leverage FP16 half-precision tensor execution, allowing your M1 Mini to process batch renders at or near real-time speeds without running out of unified memory.
